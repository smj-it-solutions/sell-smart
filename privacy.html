Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sell Smart handles information when you use our point-of-sale (POS), inventory and invoice management application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter business information such as:

  • Product names, prices, categories and stock information.
  • Sales transactions and invoice details.
  • Customer or supplier information that you choose to enter.
  • Business reports and other records you create in the app.

Avoid entering sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your business operations.

2. Local Data Storage

Sell Smart is designed to store its business records locally on your device. Under the app's local-storage design, these records are not automatically synchronized to a Sell Smart cloud database.

Anyone with access to an unlocked device or accessible app files may be able to access information stored on that device. Keep your device secure and protect your backups.

3. Backups and Exported Files

Sell Smart provides available backup and export options, including PDF and Excel exports and device or Google Drive backup functionality, where supported by your version of the app.

When you choose to create or transfer a backup, information may be stored in the destination you select. Third-party services, including Google Drive, are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

You are responsible for protecting exported files and backups, choosing secure destinations and deleting copies you no longer need.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

Sell Smart is not designed to automatically upload your local business records to a Sell Smart cloud database. Information you export, share or back up may be handled by the applications and services you choose to use.

We do not sell your locally stored business records. If future versions introduce analytics, cloud services, advertising or other data collection, this policy should be updated to explain those practices.

5. Permissions

Depending on the device and version, Sell Smart may request permissions needed for features such as barcode scanning, selecting files, creating exports or accessing backup destinations. Permission availability and behavior may differ between platforms.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device's settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, uninstall the application or remove them using the app's available controls. The exact behavior depends on the platform and app version.

Uninstalling the app may not delete exported files or backups saved separately on your device or in a cloud storage account. Manage those copies separately.

7. Children's Privacy

Sell Smart is intended as a business management tool, not as a service directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature of the app that collects it, contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Security

We aim to support responsible handling of business information. However, no device, application or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your operating system updated, secure access to your device and store backups safely.

9. Third-Party Services

Features involving third-party services, such as Google Drive, may be subject to the privacy policies and terms of those providers. Review their policies before using those services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
